    Contributor terms (draft)

    Outlines you submit become part of a community-labelled dataset. By submitting, you agree that:

    • your outlines may be released openly as part of that dataset, without your name;
    • outlines are weighted by your accuracy on expert-verified cases, and low-rated ones are reviewed by a radiologist;
    • your outlines are learning exercises, not clinical opinions.

    This is placeholder wording for the prototype. It needs legal review before launch.

    Data sources

    Every case in this prototype is synthetic. The images are drawn in the browser, so no patient data is included. Production cases would come from these datasets. Entries marked "confirm" still need checking on the owner's page.
